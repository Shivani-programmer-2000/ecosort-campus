# EcoSort Campus — User Guide for Housekeeping Staff

> **Purpose:** Helps campus housekeeping staff find the correct waste bin for any item.

---

## The Four Waste Streams

| Stream | What goes in it | App colour |
|---|---|---|
| **Wet** | Food scraps, peels, tea bags, cooked food, egg shells, flowers, garden waste | Green |
| **Dry** | Plastic bottles, paper, cardboard, glass, metal cans, old clothes, rubber, wood | Blue |
| **Sanitary** | Used sanitary pads, tampons, diapers, condoms | Orange |
| **Special care** | Batteries, tube lights, CFLs, expired medicines, paint cans, mercury thermometers | Purple |

> **Safety:** Wear gloves when handling Sanitary items. Never handle broken glass or batteries bare-handed.

Colours are as shown in the app. Check your campus bins for their actual colours.

---

## How to Use the Web App

1. **Open the app** — go to the address given by your supervisor.
2. **Type the item** — e.g. `used tea bag`, `broken tube light`, `plastic water bottle`.
3. **Press Sort it** — an answer card appears with Stream, Why, How to dispose, and Source.
4. **Give feedback (optional)** — tap **Yes / No** to help improve the system.

If the answer says **"Not sure"**, refer the item to the campus sustainability desk — do not put it in a random bin.

## Quick-Reference Examples

| Item | Stream | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Banana peel | **Wet** | Wet bin |
| Empty plastic bottle | **Dry** | Dry bin |
| Leftover canteen food | **Wet** | Wet bin |
| Used sanitary pad | **Sanitary** | Wrap securely, keep separate from other waste |
| Old AA batteries | **Special care** | Designated collection point |
| Broken tube light | **Special care** | Designated collection point |
| Tissue / napkin | **Not sure** | Ask the sustainability desk |

---

## Tips for Daily Use

- **Type only the item name.** `broken tube light` is better than a full sentence.
- **One item at a time** for the clearest answer.
- **Keep items separated at source.** Once wet mixes with dry, recyclables are contaminated.
- **Do not mix "Not sure" items** with other streams — set aside and ask your supervisor.

---

## Need Help?

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| App page does not load | Check with your supervisor for the correct address |
| Answer seems wrong | Tap **No** on feedback and note the item for the sustainability desk |
| Item not in the system | Report to the sustainability desk to have it added |
| Red error on the page | Reload; if it persists, contact your technical support team |

---

*EcoSort Campus is based on India's Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026 (MoEFCC). Answers are advisory only. For hazardous or unclear items, defer to your campus sustainability desk.*
